Transportation, leasing and fleet-service companies operate on thin margins and with many assets on the move. The shift toward electric vehicles, new environmental reporting requirements and the pressure to cut costs mean that the management system (the ERP, or Enterprise Resource Planning) stops being a simple accounting record and becomes the center of the operation.

Acumatica is a cloud ERP that brings finance, inventory, maintenance and assets together on a single platform. Here is what really matters when your business lives off its vehicles.

## The challenge: many assets, little visibility

When data is scattered across spreadsheets, the workshop and the accounting area, it is hard to answer basic business questions:

- What does it really cost to operate each vehicle (fuel or energy, maintenance, depreciation)?
- Which units are no longer profitable and should be replaced?
- Do we have the right spare parts without overstocking?

A unified ERP connects those pieces so that every decision is made with information, not intuition.

## What Acumatica solves for fleets

### Asset control and depreciation

Each vehicle is managed as an asset with its history of costs, usage and book value. That lets you see the real profitability per unit and plan fleet renewal with data.

### Planned maintenance

Schedule maintenance by mileage, hours of use or dates, and link each service order to its cost and its spare parts. Well-managed preventive maintenance reduces unexpected failures and downtime.

### Spare-parts inventory

Connect the spare-parts warehouse with service orders and purchasing, to avoid both stoppages due to missing parts and money tied up in overstock.

### Finance and costs in one place

Purchasing, accounts payable, invoicing and accounting all work on the same data, which speeds up the monthly close and gives visibility into cost by route, customer or unit.

### Sustainability reporting

More and more customers and regulators are asking for environmental information. With operational data centralized, it is easier to measure consumption, emissions and energy efficiency, and to report them in an orderly way.

## The transition to electric fleets

Adding electric vehicles changes cost assumptions: "charging" replaces fuel, the maintenance profile is different and depreciation follows a different curve. Having an ERP that models these costs lets you compare, based on real data, what makes sense for each route and each type of operation, instead of deciding blindly.
