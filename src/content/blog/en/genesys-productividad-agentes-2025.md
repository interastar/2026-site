## Introduction

In an environment where most customers expect answers in under 30 seconds, **agent productivity** is the backbone of any contact center. Genesys Cloud doesn't just automate tasks: it reinvents operational efficiency with **generative AI, extended reality (XR) and predictive analytics**. Learn how to transform metrics like AHT (average handle time) while prioritizing your teams' wellbeing.

## 1. Contextual generative AI: faster answers

The AI built into Genesys Cloud lets your agents resolve complex queries without switching platforms:

- **Real-time suggestions:** the AI analyzes history and customer context to propose personalized responses.
- **Lower AHT:** less time per ticket thanks to the voice and text assistant.
- **Guaranteed privacy:** anonymized data processed under compliance standards.

## 2. Total Experience (TX): unifying employees and customers

Beyond traditional omnichannel, Genesys Cloud offers:

- **TX dashboards:** monitor customer satisfaction (NPS) and agent engagement (eNPS) on a single screen.
- **Overload alerts:** the system anticipates workload peaks and redistributes tasks automatically.
- **Typical result:** lower agent turnover with proactive break alerts.

## 3. Immersive training with extended reality (XR)

For remote or distributed teams, Genesys Cloud enables:

- **Realistic simulations:** practice critical scenarios in controlled environments.
- **Real-time feedback:** the AI evaluates performance and suggests improvements during training.

## 4. Operational sustainability: productivity that cares for the planet

Productivity is also measured in environmental impact. Genesys Cloud includes:

- **Efficient routing:** optimized connections to data centers powered by renewable energy.
- **Paperless:** digital signatures and secure cloud storage.
- **Energy savings** when migrating legacy operations to the cloud.

## Why choose Genesys Cloud

- **Native integration** with your productivity and collaboration stack.
- **Workforce engagement management (WEM)** with wellbeing metrics.
- **Flexible model:** scale and adjust licenses to match your operation.

## Conclusion

Agent productivity depends on tools that combine **speed, ethics and sustainability**. Genesys Cloud isn't just software: it's an ecosystem where people and AI collaborate to exceed expectations.
